Summary

Key Findings

DVL Current as Leading Indicator

DVL current spikes (8σ+) consistently precede navigation stress events. Monitor ps_dvl_i for early warning of challenging conditions.

Computational Load Patterns

Highbrain current anomalies co-occur with sensor processing events. May indicate control system stress or mode transitions.

Temporal Clustering

62% of events occurred in the 10-14 minute window. Suggests a period of challenging operations requiring further investigation.
